SEOUL — SEPTEMBER 2026

Eight years in,
still unclassifiable.

(G)I-DLE closed out the summer with "Gimme Dat Love," the title track off ninth EP We Made — a chart sweep across Bugs, Melon, and QQ Music, plus a #1 worldwide-trending music video in 34 countries. It capped a run that already included a history-making, fully sold-out Taipei Dome show (36,000 fans, a first for any K-pop girl group there) on their fourth world tour, Syncopation. Between tour stops, the group also made its Lollapalooza debut in Chicago on July 31, drawing raves for a rain-soaked, 16-song festival set — and today, leader Soyeon has released her debut full-length solo album, What a Wonderful Life, eight tracks she wrote and composed herself, her first solo release in over five years. The tour's North American leg was cancelled in April so the group could manage the July comeback at a steadier pace; its Asia dates continue through a Macau finale at the end of August. This page is a working build of how (G)I-DLE Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking Neverland — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on the name: Cube Entertainment rebranded the group from "(G)I-DLE" to "i-dle" on May 2, 2025, the group's 7th anniversary. Korean and English press now use both names interchangeably, and "(G)I-DLE" remains the name most global fans still search for — which is why this wire keeps it in the title.

SEOUL — SEPT 7

Soyeon releases debut solo album "What a Wonderful Life"

The (G)I-DLE leader's first full-length solo record dropped at 6 p.m. KST — her first solo release in over five years, since 2021's "Windy." All eight tracks are credited to Soyeon as co-writer and co-composer: title track "퇴사할게여" (narrated by cartoonist Kian84, built around the idea of quitting a job to reject "stable future" pressure in favor of present happiness), plus "Bankroll," "IceBlueRabbit," "Star" (a music video Soyeon shot and edited herself), "야채 싫어 송," "Amigo" (feat. 민수, reportedly interpolating Madonna's "Like a Virgin"), "그냥 사랑하면 안 될까," and "낭만 보내기." Producers on the record include Groovyroom and R.Tee, and the physical package ships with a special incense holder.

Summarized from Korea Times and Etoday by (G)I-DLE Wire staff.

SEOUL — SEPT 2

The rollout to "What a Wonderful Life," in three moves

Soyeon built the runway to release day in public stages: an intro trailer on Aug 26, a highlight medley of the full album on Aug 31, and — days before the drop — a full live performance of the album on EBS 1TV's "Space Empathy" on Sept 2.

SEOUL — JUL 6

We Made lands; "Gimme Dat Love" sweeps the real-time and 7-day charts

The group's ninth Korean EP hit No. 1 on Bugs' real-time chart and Melon's 7-day releases chart, and No. 1 on QQ Music's Korea EP charts in China. The title track's music video also topped YouTube's worldwide trending list and charted in the Top 10 across 34 countries, while the EP entered iTunes' Top Albums in 21 countries and regions.

Summarized from SBS by (G)I-DLE Wire staff.

CHICAGO — JUL 31

(G)I-DLE makes its Lollapalooza debut, rain and all

The group played a 16-song, 60-minute headliner-caliber set on the festival's main T-Mobile stage at Grant Park, opening with a new band arrangement of "Super Lady" and giving "Morning" its live debut. Local coverage highlighted the members working the crowd in fluent English despite heavy rain, and the hashtag "#IDLEPALLOOZA" trended worldwide afterward.

Translated & summarized from Mediafine Global by (G)I-DLE Wire staff.

TAIPEI — MAR 7

First K-pop girl group to headline Taipei Dome — 36,000 fans, sold out

The Syncopation World Tour stop at Taipei Dome sold out completely, including limited-visibility seats added to meet demand, making the group the first K-pop girl group to headline a solo concert at the venue. It followed three previously sold-out shows at Taipei Arena during the group's 2024 tour.

Summarized from allkpop by (G)I-DLE Wire staff.

SEOUL — APR 20

Syncopation World Tour's North American leg confirmed cancelled

Cube Entertainment confirmed the cancellation of all scheduled Canada, US, and Mexico dates, citing "the direction of [the group's] global activities, local scheduling, and all other circumstances." The agency said it was reorganizing the leg while preparing for "higher-quality concerts," with the change also giving the group room to manage the July comeback at a steadier pace.

3.9 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"A tight, hook-forward five-tracker that trades the group's usual genre-hopping for a single, confident pop mode — and lets 'Crow' do the moody heavy lifting instead."

We Made (2026): (G)I-DLE's ninth Korean EP, led by "Gimme Dat Love." Where recent title tracks leaned into darker, genre-bending production, "Gimme Dat Love" is a brighter, more straightforwardly pop lead single — a deliberate change of register that paid off commercially, sweeping real-time and 7-day charts within its first week. Pre-release single "Crow," released three weeks earlier, carries more of the group's signature moodiness and doubles as the EP's emotional anchor.

Both Soyeon and Yuqi are credited on the songwriting side, continuing the group's longstanding emphasis on member-written material even as outside collaborators (including Kameron Glasper and Daily) shape more of the production than in earlier eras.
